Where is Volamp Elektrikals located in Ahmedabad?

Volamp Elektrikals corporate head office is located at 1753, Dhobi's Pole, Chinubhai Road, Khadia, Ahmedabad, Gujarat 380001 in the historic Gandhi Road electrical trading hub. Its central logistics & fulfillment terminal is situated at Aslali Highway, Ahmedabad.

What brands and cables does Volamp distribute in Gujarat?

Volamp Elektrikals is an authorized wholesale distributor and supplier of Polycab India, Finolex Cables, KEI Industries, Schneider Electric, Larsen & Toubro (L&T / Lauritz Knudsen), Dowells, and Legrand. Products include LT/HT XLPE armoured cables, 1500V solar DC cables, copper flexible wires, switchgear (ACB/MCCB/MCB), and chemical earthing rods.

What is the dispatch turnaround time for industrial electrical orders?

Orders within Ahmedabad and major Gujarat GIDC manufacturing zones (Sanand, Changodar, Vatva, Naroda, Ankleshwar, Dahej) benefit from same-day to 24-hour dispatch from the Aslali central warehouse. Pan-India shipments arrive within 48 to 72 hours with transit insurance.
